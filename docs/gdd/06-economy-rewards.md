# Stage 6: Economy & Rewards

## Currencies

### Coins (Primary Currency)
- Earned from every mini-game and platformer stage
- Spent on buildings, decorations, and upgrades
- Always visible at the top of the screen
- Gold color with a star emblem

### Stars (Achievement Currency)
- Earned from completing stages, streaks, and boss challenges
- Used to measure mastery — not spent
- Bronze, Silver, Gold tiers
- Displayed on the progress map next to each completed stage

### Hidden Gems (Discovery Currency)
- Earned by finding creative solutions (multiple ways to solve a problem)
- Earned by finding secrets in platformer stages
- Rare and special — used to unlock exclusive cosmetic items
- Three types: Ruby (red), Emerald (green), Sapphire (blue)

## Economy Balance

### Target Session Economy
A typical 20-minute session should earn approximately:

| Source | Estimated Coins |
|---|---|
| 4-5 mini-game problems | 25-50 |
| 1 mini-game completion bonus | 15 |
| Streaks | 5-15 |
| 1 platformer stage (every other session) | 20-35 |
| Daily login | 10 |
| **Total per session** | **75-125 coins** |

### Progression Pacing
- First building purchase: After ~2 sessions (day 1-2)
- First district complete: After ~15-20 sessions (week 2-3)
- Unlocking 3rd district: After ~30-40 sessions (month 1-2)
- Full village: After ~100-150 sessions (several months)

This pacing ensures:
- Quick early wins (dopamine, motivation)
- Steady mid-game progression (always something to save for)
- Long-term goals that keep them playing (rare items, full village)

## Star Ratings (Per Stage)

Each mini-game stage can earn up to 3 stars:

| Stars | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 star (Bronze) | Complete the stage (any accuracy) |
| 2 stars (Silver) | Complete with 80%+ accuracy |
| 3 stars (Gold) | Complete with 95%+ accuracy and under time bonus |

Stars are permanent — once earned, they stay. The child can replay stages to improve their star rating.

## Hidden Gems — Discovery Triggers

| Trigger | Gem Type |
|---|---|
| Find 2+ ways to solve the same problem | Ruby |
| Complete a problem at max speed with no scaffold | Emerald |
| Find a secret path in a platformer stage | Sapphire |
| Solve a problem using Chisanbop at the perfect moment (Tala hint) | Ruby |
| Complete a district with all gold stars | Sapphire |
| Find a pattern the game didn't explicitly teach | Emerald |

Gems are rare — a child might earn 1-2 per session at most. This makes them feel truly special.
