# Stage 5: Chisanbop Integration

## Design Philosophy

Chisanbop is treated as a **power-up**, not a lesson. The child discovers it through gameplay, not through tutorials. Teacher Tala (Tortoise) introduces it naturally, and the game rewards its use without requiring it.

---

## How Chisanbop Appears in the Game

### The School District
Teacher Tala's school is always accessible from the village map. It's framed as a "Dojo" or "Training Ground" — a place to learn secret techniques.

**First visit** (triggered at Village Level 3):
- Tala greets the child: "I have a secret trick that great Problem Solvers use..."
- Short interactive demo (30 seconds):
  - Animated hands on screen
  - "Your right hand counts ones. Each finger is 1. Your thumb is 5!"
  - 3 quick practice taps: "Show me 3" → "Show me 7" → "Show me 5"
  - Done. No long tutorial. Just enough to plant the seed.

**Subsequent visits** (optional, child-initiated):
- "Practice Mode" — Free play with the hands, no pressure
- "Challenge Mode" — Quick-fire "show me this number" for bonus coins
- "New Technique" — Unlocks at milestones:
  - Level 1: Right hand ones (0-9)
  - Level 2: Left hand tens (0-90)
  - Level 3: Both hands combined (0-99)
  - Level 4: Addition with fingers
  - Level 5: Subtraction with fingers
  - Level 6: Speed drills

---

## Chisanbop as a Power-Up in Mini-Games

### The Hand Icon
- A small hand icon sits in the corner of every mini-game screen
- It pulses gently when a problem might benefit from finger counting
- Tapping it opens the Chisanbop overlay:
  - Two animated hands appear at the bottom of the screen
  - The child can tap fingers up/down to count
  - The current finger value is displayed (e.g., "37")
  - Dismiss by tapping outside or pressing the X

### Cost / Reward Structure
- **Early levels**: Free to use. No cost. Encouraged.
- **Mid levels**: Free but no speed bonus. Using it takes time.
- **Later levels**: Using it costs 1 coin (small). Solving WITHOUT it earns bonus coins.
- **Master levels**: The icon is hidden. It's still accessible (long-press the corner), but the child should be doing mental math by now.

This creates a **natural fade**: the child uses Chisanbop heavily at first (free, helpful), then starts choosing not to use it (earns more coins without it), then doesn't need it at all (internalized).

---

## Tala's Pop-Up Tips

Teacher Tala occasionally appears in mini-games with a quick tip. These are 1-line messages, not tutorials.

**Triggers:**
- Child gets stuck on a problem for >15 seconds:
  - Tala peeks in: "Try using your fingers for this one!" (hand icon pulses)
- Child uses Chisanbop successfully 5 times:
  - Tala: "You're getting good at that! Try the next one without your fingers — I bet you can!"
- Child solves a hard problem without Chisanbop:
  - Tala: "You did that in your head! Amazing!" (+bonus coins)

**Rules for Tala tips:**
- Maximum 1 tip per mini-game session
- Never interrupts active gameplay
- Always dismissable with a tap
- Tone is encouraging, never lecturing

## Chisanbop Progression by Grade

| Grade | What's Taught | How It Appears |
|---|---|---|
| K | Not introduced yet | Hands icon not visible. Too early. |
| 1 | Right hand (0-9) | Tala teaches ones. Hand icon appears in Farm games. |
| 1-2 | Both hands (0-99) | Tala teaches tens. Hand icon in Farm + Market games. |
| 2-3 | Addition with fingers | Tala shows adding on fingers. Available in all games. |
| 3-4 | Subtraction with fingers | Tala shows subtracting. Available but costs coins. |
| 4-5 | Speed mode + fade | Quick-fire Chisanbop challenges. Icon starts hiding. |
| 5+ | Mental math | Chisanbop internalized. Rarely needed. Hidden icon. |

## The 9s Trick (Special Lesson)

When the child reaches multiplication by 9 in the Bakery, Tala offers a special visit:

**"The Secret of 9"**:
- "Did you know 9 is hiding inside 10? Watch..."
- Visual: 9 = 10 - 1
- "So 9 × 3 = (10 × 3) - 3 = 30 - 3 = 27"
- Then: the classic finger trick for 9s
  - Hold up 10 fingers
  - For 9 × 4: put down finger #4
  - Left of the gap: 3 fingers (tens). Right of the gap: 6 fingers (ones). Answer: 36.
- "But here's WHY it works..." — Quick visual showing the 10-1 pattern
- 3 practice problems
- Badge earned: "Master of 9"

This is the ONE time the game explains the math behind a trick — because the 9s trick is so magical that kids remember it forever, and understanding WHY makes it stick even harder.
