# Stage 3: Mini-Games

## Two Types of Gameplay

1. **Village Mini-Games** — Triggered by talking to villagers. Top-down or UI-based. Math is the core mechanic.
2. **Platformer Stages** — Side-scrolling action between village tasks. Math is woven into movement/collection.

---

## VILLAGE MINI-GAMES

### THE FARM (Farmer Flora — Cow)

#### 1. Crate Stacker
**Math**: Counting, making 10, addition
**How it works**:
- Apple trees shake when tapped — apples fall in random clusters (2, 3, 5, etc.)
- Open crates sit at the bottom, each holds exactly 10 apples
- Child drags apples into crates
- When a crate hits 10, it seals with a satisfying slam and slides onto a cart
- Goal: Fill as many crates as possible before the cart timer runs out
- **Why it teaches**: The child physically makes 10 over and over. They start to feel which combinations fill a crate. "I have 7 in the crate, I need 3 more" becomes instinct.

#### 2. Egg Collector
**Math**: Counting, grouping, skip counting
**How it works**:
- Chickens lay eggs around the coop (appear one at a time, randomly placed)
- Child taps eggs to collect them into baskets
- Baskets hold groups of 2, 5, or 10 (varies by level)
- Counter shows total eggs collected
- Goal: Collect the target number of eggs
- **Why it teaches**: Skip counting by grouping. Seeing that 4 baskets of 5 = 20.

#### 3. Harvest Rows
**Math**: Addition, early multiplication readiness
**How it works**:
- Crops grow in rows on the field
- Child swipes to harvest a row — counter adds that row's count
- Rows have different amounts (a row of 6 carrots, a row of 8 potatoes)
- Goal: Harvest exactly the target number. Can't undo a harvest.
- **Why it teaches**: Running totals, addition strategy ("I have 14, I need 20, which row gives me 6?")

### THE BAKERY (Baker Ben — Pig)

#### 4. Order Up
**Math**: Arrays, multiplication
**How it works**:
- Customers walk in and order: "I want a tray of cookies!"
- An empty tray appears (grid: 2x3, 3x4, 4x5, etc.)
- Child taps grid cells to place cookies — they fill one at a time
- When the tray is full, customer pays, next customer arrives
- Speed bonus: faster = more coins
- Goal: Serve as many customers as possible
- **Why it teaches**: Every tray IS an array. 3 rows of 4 = 12 cookies. They build it physically.

#### 5. Recipe Mix
**Math**: Addition, measurement, early fractions
**How it works**:
- A recipe card shows: "2 cups flour + 3 cups sugar + 1 cup butter"
- Measuring cups of different sizes sit on the counter
- Child drags the right number of cups into the mixing bowl
- Bowl shows running total
- Get it right: the batter turns into a perfect cake
- Get it wrong: the batter explodes (funny, not scary — flour everywhere)
- **Why it teaches**: Addition with context. Fractions when half-cups are introduced.

#### 6. Cookie Cutter
**Math**: Fractions, equal parts
**How it works**:
- A sheet of cookie dough appears
- The order says: "Cut this into 4 equal pieces"
- Child drags cutting lines across the dough
- Game checks if pieces are roughly equal
- Equal = each piece bakes perfectly. Unequal = some burn, some are raw (visual feedback)
- **Why it teaches**: Fractions as equal parts, visually and physically.

### THE MARKET (Merchant Mara — Raccoon)

#### 7. Make Change
**Math**: Subtraction, money, mental math
**How it works**:
- Customer approaches with an item (price shown: 37 coins)
- Customer hands over a bill (50 coins)
- Coin drawer opens — child drags coins to make correct change
- Available coins: 1s, 5s, 10s, 25s
- Correct change: customer smiles, pays tip. Wrong: customer looks confused, coins spread back.
- **Why it teaches**: Subtraction in the most practical context. Counting up strategy.

#### 8. Stock Up
**Math**: Addition, budgeting, comparison
**How it works**:
- Mara has 50 coins to spend at the wholesaler
- Items displayed with prices (apples: 8 coins, bread: 12 coins, etc.)
- Child picks items to buy, running total shown
- Can't go over budget
- Goal: Buy specific items on a shopping list, or maximize items within budget
- **Why it teaches**: Addition with a constraint. Strategic thinking.

#### 9. Fair Trade
**Math**: Multiplication, comparison, equivalence
**How it works**:
- Two villagers want to trade goods
- "I'll give you 3 bags of 6 apples for 2 bags of 9 oranges"
- Visual: bags open to show contents
- Child decides: is this fair? Thumbs up or thumbs down.
- If wrong, the game shows both sides counted out
- **Why it teaches**: Comparing products. Early multiplication reasoning.

### THE CONSTRUCTION YARD (Builder Brix — Gorilla)

#### 10. Build the Wall
**Math**: Addition, place value, counting
**How it works**:
- A blueprint shows: "Wall needs to be 24 blocks"
- Block bundles available: singles (1), stacks of 10
- Child drags blocks to build the wall from left to right
- Wall visually grows with each block placed
- Goal: Exact count. Too many = wall is too long, it cracks.
- **Why it teaches**: Place value (tens and ones), composing numbers.

#### 11. Bridge Builder
**Math**: Number bonds, decomposition, subtraction
**How it works**:
- A gap between two cliff edges (shows the distance: e.g., 15 units)
- Plank pieces available in different lengths (2, 3, 5, 7, 10)
- Child drags planks to span the gap — they snap together
- Must be exact. Too short = planks fall. Too long = they stick out and break.
- Multiple solutions possible (5+10, 3+5+7, etc.) — hidden gem for finding more than one
- **Why it teaches**: Decomposition, number bonds, flexible thinking.

#### 12. Blueprint Match
**Math**: Measurement, comparison, subtraction
**How it works**:
- A blueprint shows a structure with dimensions
- "This room needs to be 8 blocks wide and 5 blocks tall"
- Child builds it block by block in a 2D grid
- A measurement tool shows current dimensions vs. target
- Goal: Match the blueprint exactly
- **Why it teaches**: Spatial reasoning, counting, area concept.

### THE FESTIVAL GROUNDS (Festival Director Fizz — Monkey)

#### 13. Set the Tables
**Math**: Division, equal groups
**How it works**:
- Fizz announces: "48 guests are coming! Tables seat 6 each!"
- Empty tables appear in the field
- Villager icons line up on the side
- Child drags villagers to tables — table shows count
- When a table is full (6), it lights up with decorations
- Goal: Seat all guests with no one left standing (or figure out the remainder)
- **Why it teaches**: Division as equal grouping, with visible remainders.

#### 14. Hang the Lanterns
**Math**: Skip counting, patterns, multiplication
**How it works**:
- A string stretches across the festival grounds
- "Place a lantern every 3 feet" (string is 24 feet)
- Child taps the string at regular intervals to hang lanterns
- Visual ruler shows the string length
- Wrong spacing: lanterns are uneven, string sags
- Correct: lanterns glow beautifully, even spacing
- **Why it teaches**: Skip counting with visual pattern. Connects to multiplication.

#### 15. Prize Booth
**Math**: Patterns, sequences, logical thinking
**How it works**:
- Prizes on shelves follow a pattern (red, blue, red, blue, ?, ?)
- Child picks the next items in the sequence
- Patterns get more complex: color, shape, number, then combined
- Goal: Complete the pattern to win the prize
- **Why it teaches**: Pattern recognition — foundational to all math.

### THE HARBOR (Captain Kai — Parrot)

#### 16. Load the Ship
**Math**: Place value, addition to 100
**How it works**:
- Ship's cargo hold has a capacity (e.g., 100 units)
- Crates come in sizes: 1, 5, 10, 25
- Child loads crates onto the ship — weight counter rises
- Overload = ship sinks (funny animation, bubbles, Kai flapping)
- Goal: Load exactly the target weight
- **Why it teaches**: Place value, strategic addition, estimation.

#### 17. Voyage Tracker
**Math**: Fractions, parts of a whole
**How it works**:
- A map shows the voyage from Island A to Island B
- The journey is divided into segments (e.g., 8 segments)
- Ship has sailed 3 segments. "How far have we gone? How far is left?"
- Child answers by tapping a fraction bar or dragging a marker
- Visual: the ship moves along the route as they answer
- **Why it teaches**: Fractions as parts of a journey — concrete context.

#### 18. Treasure Split
**Math**: Division, equal sharing, remainders
**How it works**:
- Treasure chest opens: 36 gold coins!
- Crew members stand in a line (e.g., 6 crew)
- Child drags coins to distribute equally
- Any remaining coins go back in the chest
- Goal: Fair shares for everyone
- **Why it teaches**: Division with physical manipulation. Remainders are tangible.

### THE BANK (Banker Bloom — Great Horned Owl)

#### 19. Coin Counter
**Math**: Decimals, place value
**How it works**:
- A pile of mixed coins (dollars and cents) on the counter
- Child sorts coins into columns: dollars, dimes, pennies
- Place value chart fills as coins are sorted
- Total displays as a decimal: $4.73
- Goal: Count the exact total
- **Why it teaches**: Decimal place value through physical sorting.

#### 20. Budget Planner
**Math**: Mixed operations, multi-step
**How it works**:
- The village needs to buy supplies for a project
- Budget: $50.00
- Shopping list with prices, quantities needed
- Child calculates totals: "3 bags of cement at $8 each = ?"
- Checks if it fits the budget, makes trade-offs
- **Why it teaches**: Multi-step operations in a meaningful context.

#### 21. Vault Combo
**Math**: Order of operations, number puzzles
**How it works**:
- The vault door has a number lock
- A target number is shown (e.g., 24)
- Available numbers and operations: 3, 4, 8, +, -, ×
- Child arranges them to make the target: 3 × 8 = 24
- Multiple solutions earn hidden gems
- **Why it teaches**: Number sense, operations flexibility, order of operations.

## PLATFORMER STAGES

Platformer stages appear between village tasks as bonus content. They use side-scrolling mechanics with math woven into the action.

### Stage Type 1: Number Run
**Math**: Addition, target numbers
**How it works**:
- Character runs automatically (auto-runner)
- Platforms appear with numbers on them
- A target number is shown at the top (e.g., "Collect: 15")
- Child jumps to platforms to collect number values
- Running total displayed
- Must hit exactly the target to pass through the finish gate
- Overshooting: gate stays shut, must restart section

### Stage Type 2: Coin Dash
**Math**: Counting, grouping
**How it works**:
- Traditional platformer — child controls run and jump
- Coins scattered across platforms (1s, 5s, 10s marked)
- Obstacles to jump over (doesn't hurt, just slows down)
- Goal: collect exactly the target amount before reaching the end
- Timer adds pressure but doesn't kill

### Stage Type 3: Sky Tower
**Math**: Skip counting, sequences
**How it works**:
- Vertical platformer — climbing upward
- Platforms are numbered
- Only the correct sequence platforms are solid (3, 6, 9, 12...)
- Wrong number platforms crumble when landed on (fall back to last correct one)
- Goal: climb to the top by following the pattern

### Stage Type 4: Bridge Dash
**Math**: Subtraction, number bonds
**How it works**:
- Running left to right
- Gaps in the ground with numbers (the gap "width")
- Plank dispensers give planks of specific lengths
- Must grab the right plank and place it at the gap to cross
- Wrong plank = falls through (bounces back, doesn't die)
- Fast-paced with increasing difficulty

### Stage Type 5: Boss Run
**Math**: Mixed operations, everything learned so far
**How it works**:
- Longer stage combining elements of all platformer types
- Appears at the end of each district
- Must complete to fully "clear" the district
- Bigger rewards: rare gems, unlockable characters, cosmetics
- No time limit — but speed earns bonus coins

## BOSS CHALLENGES

Each district has a boss challenge that appears after enough mini-games are completed.

### How Boss Challenges Work
- The district NPC announces a big event (Festival, Harvest, Grand Opening, etc.)
- 5-7 rapid-fire problems mixing everything learned in that district
- Visual progress bar fills with each correct answer
- Wrong answer: bar doesn't move, but no penalty — just try the next one
- Complete the bar: massive celebration, major rewards
- A platformer Boss Run stage unlocks after completing the village boss

### Boss Challenge Rewards
- 50-100 bonus coins
- 3 gold stars
- Unlockable character (for specific districts)
- District visual upgrade (buildings get decorations, flowers bloom, etc.)
- Next district hint revealed (fog starts to sparkle)
