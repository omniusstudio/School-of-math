# Kenney.nl Asset Research for Countopia

> Research date: 2026-04-06
> All Kenney assets are **free** and **CC0 licensed** (public domain, no attribution required).

---

## Summary

Kenney.nl offers strong coverage for **UI elements**, **game objects/icons**, **sound effects**, and **short music jingles**. It has partial coverage for **characters** and **tilesets**. It has significant gaps for Countopia's specific needs around **custom cartoon village environments**, **math tool visuals**, **avatar customization pieces**, and **full background music tracks**.

---

### Recommended Packs

| Pack | Assets | URL | Notes |
|------|--------|-----|-------|
| **Toon Characters 1** | 270 | https://kenney.nl/assets/toon-characters-1 | Best fit -- cartoon style characters with various poses. Could serve as NPC villagers or base inspiration. |
| **Platformer Characters** | 150 | https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-characters | Multiple characters with standing, jumping, ducking, walking, climbing, swimming poses. 15+ enemies with animations. |

| **Animal Pack** | 80 | https://kenney.nl/assets/animal-pack | Simpler animal sprites. |

### Coverage Assessment
- **Covered**: Generic cartoon characters, basic walking/standing poses, some enemy/NPC variety.
- **NOT Covered**: Countopia's specific NPC characters (Farmer Flora, Baker Ben, Captain Kai, etc.), the chibi avatar system with 152 mix-and-match pieces (body bases, hair layers, face expressions, outfits, accessories), diverse skin tones, or the specific villager designs. These are highly custom and would still need AI generation or commissioned art.

## 3. BACKGROUNDS & TILESETS (Village Scenes)

### Recommended Packs

| Pack | Assets | URL | Notes |
|------|--------|-----|-------|
| **Tiny Town** | 130 | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town | Buildings, objects, terrain, foliage for overworld/town creation. Top-down style. Best match for village building. |
| **Platformer Art Deluxe** | 930 | https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-art-deluxe | Massive set of platformer tiles -- ground, structures, objects. Side-view perspective. |
| **Platformer Pack Redux** | 360 | https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-pack-redux | Clean platformer tileset with varied terrain. |
| **Pixel Platformer** | 200 | https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer | Pixel-art platformer tiles (style may not match). |

### Coverage Assessment
- **Partially Covered**: Tiny Town gives generic buildings and terrain tiles that could serve as placeholder or inspiration for the village map. Platformer packs provide ground/terrain tiles.
- **NOT Covered**: Countopia needs 9 specific, richly detailed scene backgrounds each with 3 parallax layers (foreground, midground, background) -- farm with barn/windmill/crops, market with stalls, bakery interior, construction yard, school interior, festival grounds, harbor with ships, bank interior, and a village overview map. These are all highly specific custom scenes. Kenney's tile-based approach cannot replicate the full illustrated backgrounds described in `environments.md`. **This is the biggest gap.**

### Note on Fantasy Town Kit
The **Fantasy Town Kit** (160 assets, https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit) is **3D models** (FBX/OBJ/GLTF), not 2D sprites. Not directly usable for Countopia's 2D art style, though Asset Forge could render 2D sprites from them.

## 5. AUDIO / MUSIC / SFX

### Recommended Packs

| Pack | Assets | URL | Notes |
|------|--------|-----|-------|
| **UI Audio** | 50 | https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio | Button clicks, switches, generic UI sounds. Covers button click, menu open/close. |
| **Interface Sounds** | 100 | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds | More UI/interface sound effects. |
| **Digital Audio** | 60 | https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio | Digital/electronic sound effects. |
| **Impact Sounds** | 130 | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds | Impact/collision sounds -- could cover drop/place, crate filling. |
| **Music Jingles** | 85 | https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles | Short musical jingles -- could cover level complete fanfare, achievement sounds, building unlocked. |
| **RPG Audio** | 50 | https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio | RPG-themed sounds -- coins, chests, discovery sounds. |

### Coverage Assessment
- **Well Covered**: Button clicks, UI navigation sounds (menu open/close), generic positive/negative feedback tones, coin collect sounds, impact sounds, short celebratory jingles.
- **Partially Covered**: Music Jingles could serve for level-complete fanfares and achievement moments. RPG Audio may have chest-opening and coin sounds. Impact Sounds may cover drag/drop/place mechanics.
- **NOT Covered**:
  - **Background music tracks** (10 unique loopable tracks for each location) -- Kenney has Music Jingles (short) but not full looping background tracks in the specific moods needed (pastoral farm, busy market, cozy bakery, etc.). Need AI music generation (Suno/Udio) or royalty-free music libraries.
  - **Skip counting songs** (5 vocal songs) -- completely custom, need AI or human vocal recording.
  - **Character-specific sounds** (villager happy/worried/greeting in Animal Crossing style) -- not in Kenney's library.
  - **Timer ticking** loop, **streak** ascending notes, **map zoom** swoosh -- may exist approximately but need to be verified.
