# Environments & Backgrounds — AI Art Generation Prompts

> **Style Guide**: 2D cartoon, clean lines, flat color with subtle cel-shading, bright saturated palette. Each background should have 3 depth layers (foreground, midground, background) for potential parallax scrolling. Wide format: 1920x1080px. Transparent foreground elements where noted.

## Background Villagers (Ambient NPCs)

Generic villager sprites that walk around to make the village feel alive. Small on screen, less detail needed.

### Prompt Template

```
2D cartoon small villager character, [DESCRIPTION], simple design, limited detail suitable for
background character, standing idle pose, chibi proportions, clean black outlines, flat color,
transparent background, 256x256px
```

| # | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | `elderly woman, dark skin, white hair in a bun, wearing a green shawl and brown dress` |
| 2 | `young boy, light skin, blonde messy hair, wearing a red striped shirt and blue shorts` |
| 3 | `young girl, medium-dark skin, black pigtails with ribbons, wearing a pink dress` |
| 4 | `middle-aged man, medium skin, brown beard, wearing a brown vest and white shirt` |
| 5 | `teenage girl, East Asian features, straight black hair, wearing a blue jacket and skirt` |
| 6 | `elderly man, light skin, bald with white mustache, wearing suspenders and a flat cap` |
| 7 | `young woman, dark skin, curly hair with headband, wearing an orange top and jeans` |
| 8 | `middle-aged woman, medium-light skin, red hair in a bob, wearing a purple apron and dress` |
| 9 | `young boy, deep dark skin, short hair, wearing a green hoodie and grey pants` |
| 10 | `teenage boy, South Asian features, dark wavy hair, wearing a yellow polo and khaki pants` |

Each villager also needs a **walking pose** variant:

```
Same as above but in a mid-step walking pose, one foot forward, arms swinging slightly, natural
walking motion, same style and size
```

**Total ambient villagers: 10 characters x 2 poses = 20 sprites**

## File Naming Convention

```
env_[location]_bg.png          (background layer)
env_[location]_mg.png          (midground layer)
env_[location]_fg.png          (foreground layer)
env_village_map.png            (overview map)
env_fog_overlay.png            (lockable area fog)
villager_bg_[number]_idle.png  (background villager idle)
villager_bg_[number]_walk.png  (background villager walking)
```
